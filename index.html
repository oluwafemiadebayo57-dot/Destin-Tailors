The Brand

Cut2Fit. A standard of excellence.

Destin Tailors is a leading Nigerian clothing brand based in Akure. We believe that true elegance starts with a perfect fit. Whether you are walking down the aisle, attending a high-profile event, or updating your everyday wardrobe, we bring precision to every stitch.

With over 79 five-star reviews on Google, our reputation is built on trust, attention to detail, and an unwavering commitment to making you look your absolute best.

  • Master tailors for Men's, Women's & Children's wear
  • Specialists in luxury suits and bespoke groomswear
  • Expert alterations, restyling, and general repairs
  • Convenient location at Masters Energy Complex, Oba Ile Rd

How it works

Three fittings from idea to wardrobe.

01

Consultation

We discuss the occasion, your style preferences, and fabric choices. We take precise measurements to establish the perfect fit.

02

Measurement & Cut

Your pattern is drafted from scratch (never from a size chart). The fabric is cut by hand with meticulous attention to grain and detail.

03

Fitting & Delivery

You try on the garment. We make any necessary refinements, press the final piece, and package it beautifully for pickup or delivery.
